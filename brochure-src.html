01 · What we do

Numbers you can actually run the business on

Most small businesses see their real position once a year — at tax time, when it's far too late to act on it. We close your books every month, so the decisions happen while they still matter.

  • Transactions reconciled and GST-coded, every month — not reconstructed in October
  • Monthly profit & loss, balance sheet and budget-vs-actual reporting
  • Tax-ready books and a year-end pack your accountant will thank you for
  • BAS-ready figures each cycle, prepared for your registered agent to lodge
  • Receipts and documents captured against the transactions they belong to
  • Questions answered in the portal chat — not in six weeks

Before the call

What we'll ask you for

Nothing needs to be tidy — that's our job. Having these to hand just makes the first conversation faster and the quote more accurate.

  • Your records, as they are — the last few months of bank and card statements, or read-only access to your accounting file. Shoeboxes accepted.
  • Your entity details — ABN, business structure, whether you're registered for GST and how often you report it.
  • Any software you already use — Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks or a spreadsheet. We work with what you have, or set something up properly.
  • Who lodges for you — your registered tax or BAS agent, if you have one. If you don't, we can point you to good ones.

05 · Straight talk

We prepare. Your registered agent lodges.

ApexLedger is a bookkeeping and management-accounting practice. We are not currently a registered tax or BAS agent, and we do not lodge. What we do is make lodgement easy — GST-coded books, BAS-ready figures and a year-end pack prepared to professional standard, handed to the registered agent of your choice. If you don't have one, we can point you to good ones. Tax and BAS lodgement services are on our roadmap, pending registration.

Your data is held encrypted in Australia (Sydney), with row-level isolation so each client can only ever reach their own records, and sensitive details such as tax file numbers are vault-encrypted — never stored in plain text.

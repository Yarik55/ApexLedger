Straight talk

We prepare. Your registered agent lodges.

ApexLedger is a bookkeeping and management-accounting practice — we are not currently a registered tax or BAS agent, and we don't lodge. What we do is make lodgement easy: GST-coded books, BAS-ready figures and a year-end pack prepared to professional standard, handed to the registered agent of your choice. Tax and BAS lodgement services are on our roadmap — pending registration. Your data lives encrypted in Sydney, and sensitive details like TFNs are vault-encrypted, never stored in plain text.

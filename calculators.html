Do I need to register?

GST registration check

Registration is required once your GST turnover meets the threshold — $75,000, or $150,000 for not-for-profits (s. 23-15). Taxi and ride-share drivers must register from the first dollar (Div 144).

GST turnover has its own definition (it excludes GST itself, input-taxed supplies and some other items) — based on your inputs only. Registering can also be worthwhile voluntarily to claim credits; that's a conversation, not a calculator.

Quick guide

Not everything has GST in it

The GST Act sorts supplies into three buckets — and the bucket decides both the GST on your sales and the credits on your purchases.

Taxable — 10%

The default (s. 9-5): most goods and services sold by a GST-registered business in Australia. You charge 10% GST and claim credits on related purchases.

GST-free — 0%, credits OK

No GST on the sale, but you still claim input tax credits (Div 38): most basic food, most health and medical services, most education courses, and exports.

Input-taxed — 0%, no credits

No GST on the sale and no credits on related purchases (Div 40): financial supplies (lending, shares) and residential rent are the big two.

How Division 7A bites
  • No complying loan agreement in place by the company's lodgment day → the whole loan can be a deemed, unfrankable dividend (s. 109D).
  • Complying loan but repayment short → the shortfall below the minimum yearly repayment is the deemed dividend (s. 109E).
  • Deemed dividends are capped at the company's distributable surplus.
  • Repayments made by simply borrowing again from the company generally don't count.
  • Payments and debt forgiveness by a private company can be caught too — not just loans.

Before you pay FBT, check the exemptions
  • Minor benefits — under $300 (GST-inclusive), provided infrequently and irregularly → generally exempt (ch 20).
  • Work tools — portable devices, protective clothing, tools of trade used mainly for work → exempt (ch 20).
  • Otherwise deductible — if the employee could have claimed the expense as a deduction themselves, the taxable value drops by that portion (declaration required).
  • Records — logbooks (12 weeks, valid 5 FBT years), odometer readings, employee declarations. Good records are the whole game — that's the part we do.
